  <section class="report-section">
    <span class="section-kicker">Section 1</span>
    <h2>Company Snapshot &amp; Business Architecture</h2>
    <p>
      RACL Geartech is a 42-year-old, Noida-headquartered precision engineering company manufacturing ultra-precision gears, shafts, sub-assemblies, and chassis components. The company operates in a specialised tier of the automotive value chain: it procures specialized alloy steel, machines it into sub-micron finished gear components, and supplies directly to Tier-1 system integrators (ZF, Bosch) and premium marquee OEMs (BMW, Royal Enfield, TVS, KTM, Kubota/Escorts, Kawasaki, MAN Trucks, BRP Canada).
    </p>
    <p>
      Roughly two-thirds of revenue is exported—predominantly to Europe (~59–69% of sales)—giving it a 16-year-old, asset-backed presence in the region with 5 strategically located warehouses. This logistics infrastructure represents a critical competitive moat that cannot be easily replicated by newer Indian competitors trying to crack European OEM supply chains.
    </p>

    <div class="callout green">
      <strong>Core Moat Dynamics:</strong> Precision transmission manufacturing is not a commoditized fabrication business. OEM vendor qualification cycles take 24 to 36 months, requiring micron-level gear-grinding tolerances, metallurgical heat-treatment certifications, and rigorous field durability testing. RACL functions as a sole-source or majority-source supplier for over 80% of its customer part numbers, resulting in near-zero customer attrition across four decades.
    </div>
  </section>

  <!-- Section 2 -->
  <section class="report-section">
    <span class="section-kicker">Section 2</span>
    <h2>Guidance Check — What Management Has Actually Committed To</h2>
    <p>
      Management guidance is disciplined and backed by concrete strategic roadmaps. Historically, management’s 2019 five-year internal strategy document (<strong>‘RACL 3.0’</strong>) accurately forecasted the entry into passenger cars, the structural reduction of 3-wheeler dependency, and the R&amp;D partnership with ARRK in Munich.
    </p>
    <p>
      Management is currently executing the ₹1,000 Cr → ₹2,000 Cr roadmap. For FY27, management has formally guided:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>FY27 Revenue Guidance:</strong> ₹565 Cr ± 5% (representing 15–20% annual compounding off a higher-margin base).</li>
      <li><strong>FY27 Standalone EBITDA Margin:</strong> Guided in the high-24% to 25.5% corridor (Q1FY27 printed at 25.3%).</li>
      <li><strong>Total FY27 Capex:</strong> ₹77.45 Cr, anchored by a ₹43 Cr modernization of the in-house metallurgical heat-treatment facility.</li>
      <li><strong>Long-term Vision:</strong> Credible operational path to doubling revenue by FY29–FY31 without diluting return ratios.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="report-section">
    <span class="section-kicker">Section 3</span>
    <h2>Core Growth Triggers (Detailed Breakdown)</h2>

    <!-- Trigger 1 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">1. Heat-Treatment Plant Overhaul — Capacity + Cost + ESG</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge immed">Trial Jan-2027 · FY28+ Full Benefit</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Gears must undergo heat-treatment (hardening) after hobbing and cutting; it is the single most critical, least outsourceable phase in gear manufacturing that dictates noise, vibration, and fatigue life. RACL is entirely replacing its legacy furnaces with modern, all-electric, computerized vacuum-furnace installations.
      </p>
      <div class="sub-block">
        <span class="sub-block-label">Financial &amp; Operational Impact</span>
        <p>
          Allocates ≈₹43 Cr of the ₹77.45 Cr FY27 capex. Delivers a structural <strong>200–300 bps EBITDA margin expansion</strong> by eliminating external job-work, expanding in-house hardening capacity to 1,500 MT/month, cutting scrap rates, and meeting European OEM ESG emission audits.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="sub-block">
        <span class="sub-block-label">Timeline</span>
        <p>Trial production starts in January 2027; full operational margin benefit flows structurally into FY28 and beyond.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 2 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">2. BMW Electric-Vehicle Gearbox Programs (Titan &amp; Venus)</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge immed">Mass Production Oct-2026</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        RACL was nominated by BMW as the sole-source supplier for two distinct EV powertrain platforms (internally designated Titan and Venus). EV gearboxes operate at 15,000–20,000 RPM (vs 6,000 RPM in ICE), requiring extreme micron tolerances to minimize high-frequency gear whine.
      </p>
      <div class="sub-block">
        <span class="sub-block-label">Quantified Impact</span>
        <p>
          Described as one of the two largest capacity programs undertaken by RACL. Opens direct validation credentials with other premium European and North American EV manufacturers.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="sub-block">
        <span class="sub-block-label">Timeline</span>
        <p>Final customer sign-off scheduled for 24-Oct-2026. First full quarter of commercial revenue lands in Q3 FY27, with FY28 representing the first full year of scaled run-rate delivery.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 3 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">3. Royal Enfield — Large New Domestic OEM Scale-Up</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">Ongoing · Scale-up FY27–28</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Royal Enfield historically manufactured transmission gears in-house. In January 2026, Royal Enfield nominated RACL to supply transmission gears for its core 350cc engine platform (Classic, Bullet, Hunter)—RE's highest volume bread-and-butter platform.
      </p>
      <div class="sub-block">
        <span class="sub-block-label">Quantified Impact</span>
        <p>
          Currently supplying 7,500–8,000 units/month against a formal nomination of 10,000–20,000 units/month. Represents <strong>60% to 100%+ volume headroom</strong> on this single platform. A second, separate Royal Enfield program is in active discussion.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 4 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">4. Kawasaki — Multi-Part Platform Evolution</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge mid">Q4FY27 / FY28 Mass Prod</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        RACL is transitioning from supplying a single token part to validating a complete 15-part multi-application platform for Kawasaki. 1 part is currently live in production, with 14 parts across advanced validation phases.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 5 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">5. ZF Electric Power Steering — First US Commercial Truck Beachhead</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">SOP Sep-Oct 2026</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        RACL will manufacture precision steering gears in India; ZF will assemble the complete EPS unit at its facility in Mexico for delivery to a US Class 8 commercial vehicle OEM. Electric power steering is an absolute prerequisite for commercial truck ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems).
      </p>
      <div class="sub-block">
        <span class="sub-block-label">Strategic Importance</span>
        <p>Establishes RACL's first-ever footprint into the massive North American commercial truck market, creating a structural gateway for multi-year platform conversions.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 6 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">6. BRP Canada — Shift-Drum New Product Line</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">150K Units/Yr · Ramp FY27-28</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Enters a completely new product category: complex motorcycle and off-road vehicle shift-drum mechanisms for Bombardier Recreational Products (BRP Canada). Initial volume of 150,000 units/year for part 1, with 6 to 7 subsequent part numbers to follow.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 7 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">7. KTM (Austria) Recovery — European De-Risking</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">FY27–FY28 Re-Acceleration</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        KTM, historically RACL's largest customer, experienced temporary European channel inventory destocking in FY25. As KTM ramps its 2027 model year platform, volumes are re-accelerating. Management estimates up to 5 percentage points of FY27 revenue growth (≈₹28 Cr swing) is driven by the KTM recovery alone.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 8 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">8. Passenger-Car Mix Shift (‘RACL 3.0’) — Structural Margin Upgrade</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">Structural &amp; Ongoing</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Passenger-car revenue mix has structurally expanded from 0% in FY22–23 to <strong>13% of total revenue</strong> in FY26, while commercial vehicle components rose from 2% to 20%. Higher-tolerance passenger car components command higher gross margins, lifting standalone EBITDA margins from 22.5% to 25.3%.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 9 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">9. Balance-Sheet Deleveraging &amp; Interest Optimization</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">Realised &amp; Active</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Gross debt reduced from ₹297.6 Cr to ₹221.8 Cr over FY25–FY26. Interest coverage expanded from 3.01x to 4.26x, providing balance sheet flexibility to self-fund the ongoing ₹77.45 Cr capex program with minimal debt addition.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 10 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">10. EU-India FTA &amp; China+1 Supply Chain Realignment</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge mid">FTA 1-Jan-2027 · Long-Term Inflow</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Central European OEMs are accelerating RFQ/RFI quoting cycles to diversify sourcing away from China. India's integrated steel ecosystem, green energy competitive position, and RACL's 16-year European presence position it as a primary beneficiary.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 11 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">11. New Vertical Optionality — Aerospace, Robotics, Defence (BHEL)</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge mid">BHEL Active · Others 1–2 Yrs</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        RACL is actively incubating precision mechanical gearing solutions across high-margin non-auto verticals: BHEL defence vendor qualification is live and generating preliminary revenue; precision harmonic drives for robotics and aerospace actuators are in prototype validation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Trigger 12 -->
    <div class="trigger-card">
      <div class="trigger-header">
        <h3 class="trigger-title">12. Kubota–Escorts Working Capital Realignment</h3>
        <span class="trigger-badge near">Cash-Flow Positive</span>
      </div>
      <p>
        Kubota's acquisition of Escorts in India enables RACL to invoice Kubota parts domestically via Escorts rather than overseas export. Domestic invoicing significantly compresses receivable cycles and accelerates working capital cash conversion.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>
