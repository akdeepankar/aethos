## 1. Company snapshot

RACL Geartech is a 42-year-old, Noida-headquartered precision auto-component manufacturer. It produces gears, shafts and gearbox internals used in two-wheelers, three-wheelers, passenger cars, commercial vehicles, tractors, ATVs and selected industrial and defence equipment.

The company buys steel and converts it into finished or sub-assembled precision components through gear cutting, machining, heat treatment and finishing. Its customer base includes Tier-1 suppliers and OEMs such as **BMW, ZF, Royal Enfield, TVS, KTM Austria, Kubota-Escorts, Kawasaki, MAN Trucks and BRP Canada**.

Approximately two-thirds of revenue is exported, primarily to Europe. RACL has spent around 16 years building its European presence, supported by five warehouses.

## 2. Management guidance: what is formally committed

### FY27 revenue guidance

- **February 2026:** Management introduced FY27 revenue guidance of **₹565 crore ±5%**, implying a range of approximately ₹537-593 crore, along with ₹77.45 crore of capex.
- **June 2026:** FY26 standalone revenue closed at ₹500.22 crore, up 17% YoY. FY27 guidance was reiterated.
- **August 2026:** Q1 FY27 revenue reached ₹127.82 crore, up 18.4% YoY. Management declined to increase full-year guidance, explaining that guidance is reviewed annually and that the ±5% band absorbs business variability.

RACL needs to average approximately **₹146 crore per quarter over Q2-Q4 FY27** to reach the ₹565 crore midpoint. Historically stronger Q3 seasonality makes the target achievable, but execution across simultaneous ramps is critical.

<div class="aw-card aw-gold">
  <h3>Long-term marker—not formal guidance</h3>
  <p>Management has articulated an aspiration to reach approximately <strong>₹1,000 crore of revenue in the next three to five years</strong>, implying FY29-FY31. Only ₹565 crore ±5% for FY27 is a formally communicated number; the ₹1,000 crore ambition remains directional.</p>
</div>

## 3. Core growth triggers

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 1 · Heat-treatment plant overhaul</h3>
  <p><span class="aw-label">What is changing</span><br>RACL is replacing its roughly 30-year-old LPG-based heat-treatment furnace at Gajraula with an all-electric plant and creating in-house heat-treatment capacity at Noida, where work is currently outsourced.</p>
  <p><span class="aw-label">Why it matters</span><br>Heat treatment is a critical and difficult-to-outsource stage. Electric furnaces should reduce batch time, lower LPG exposure, improve emissions compliance and remove third-party processing margin at Noida. The existing furnace reportedly operates at only 30-40% utilisation because of downtime and maintenance.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>FY27 HT-related capex</strong><span>~₹43 Cr</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Trial production</strong><span>January 2027</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Full benefit</strong><span>FY28 onward</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 2 · BMW EV gearbox programs — Titan and Venus</h3>
  <p>RACL has qualified to supply gears used in the reduction gearboxes of two BMW electric-vehicle programs. Project Titan entered initial commercial production at the Udhyam facility in April 2026; Project Venus has also progressed through multiple validation phases.</p>
  <p><span class="aw-positive"><strong>Strategic importance:</strong></span> this is proof that RACL can qualify directly with a global luxury EV OEM. Automotive parts are often sole-sourced for the platform life, creating customer stickiness and improving the probability of future nominations.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Final customer sign-off</strong><span>24 October 2026</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Mass production</strong><span>From Q3 FY27</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>First full run-rate year</strong><span>FY28</span></div>
  </div>
  <p><span class="aw-watch"><strong>Disclosure gap:</strong></span> revenue potential and per-kit realisation remain undisclosed.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 3 · Royal Enfield — large domestic scale-up</h3>
  <p>Royal Enfield nominated RACL to supply gears for the five-speed gearbox of its high-volume 350cc engine platform. Commercial supply started in January 2026.</p>
  <p>RACL has reached approximately **7,500-8,000 sets per month**, versus an original nomination of 10,000 units and a later customer request to prepare for 20,000 units per month.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Current run-rate</strong><span>7.5K-8K sets/month</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Base nomination</strong><span>10K/month</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Stretch request</strong><span>20K/month</span></div>
  </div>
  <p>A second Royal Enfield project is being discussed but is not included in the base case.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 4 · Kawasaki — 15-part, multi-application program</h3>
  <p>Kawasaki Japan has nominated RACL for 15 components for a general-purpose engine used across sports-utility vehicles, motorcycles up to 200cc and lawn equipment. One part has started production; the remaining parts are in pilot-lot and validation stages.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Parts nominated</strong><span>15</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Already live</strong><span>1 part</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Expected mass ramp</strong><span>Q4 FY27-FY28</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 5 · ZF electric power steering — entry into US trucks</h3>
  <p>ZF is developing an electric power-steering system for North American commercial trucks, and RACL has been nominated to supply its gearbox. RACL manufactures in India, ZF assembles in Mexico and the finished system goes to a US commercial-vehicle OEM.</p>
  <p><span class="aw-info"><strong>The near-term pilot is small; the strategic value is market entry.</strong></span> Electric steering is important for ADAS-equipped trucks, giving RACL exposure to a multi-year structural transition from hydraulic to electric systems.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Pilot SOP</strong><span>September-October 2026</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Current US exposure</strong><span>~2-5% of revenue</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Project Crystal</strong><span>Commercial supply by end-2027/mid-2028</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 6 · BRP Canada — new shift-drum product line</h3>
  <p>BRP Canada nominated RACL to produce a transmission shift drum—its first meaningful product outside traditional gears and shafts. The initial sample approval process is complete.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Initial volume</strong><span>150,000 units/year</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Follow-on pipeline</strong><span>6-7 part numbers</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Program ramp</strong><span>FY27-FY28</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 7 · KTM Austria recovery</h3>
  <p>KTM's 2024 financial stress and excess dealer inventory materially affected RACL. Dealer inventory has since reportedly normalised from around 200,000 units to approximately 20,000, and management says ordering visibility has improved toward pre-COVID levels.</p>
  <p>Management built conservative KTM assumptions into FY27 guidance. Consequently, a stronger recovery could become a positive earnings surprise.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Potential guidance swing</strong><span>Up to ~5 percentage points</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Indicative revenue swing</strong><span>~₹28 Cr</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Timing</strong><span>Already flowing; ramp through FY27-FY28</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 8 · Passenger-car mix shift — RACL 3.0</h3>
  <p>RACL is moving from lower-value two-wheeler and tractor components toward precision passenger-car and commercial-vehicle work.</p>

  <p><span class="aw-positive"><strong>Why it matters:</strong></span> BMW and ZF programs carry greater engineering and quality requirements, supporting better pricing and a structurally richer margin mix.</p>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 9 · Balance-sheet deleveraging</h3>
  <p>Fresh equity was used to reduce long-term debt. Lower finance cost drove a disproportionate improvement in PBT and PAT relative to EBITDA.</p>
  <div class="aw-three">
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Total debt</strong><span>₹297.6 Cr → ₹221.8 Cr</span></div>
    <div class="aw-mini"><strong>Debt reduction</strong><span>₹75.8 Cr</span></div>

  </div>
  <p><span class="aw-watch"><strong>Important:</strong></span> FY27 capex is expected to require fresh bank borrowing, so part of the deleveraging benefit may reverse.</p>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 10 · EU-India FTA and China+1</h3>
  <p>The EU-India FTA is expected to apply from 1 January 2027. Management also reports higher RFQ activity as European manufacturers examine India as an ESG-compliant, non-China alternative for precision mechanical components.</p>
  <p>RACL's long-standing European relationships and five-warehouse network provide an advantage over Indian suppliers starting without an installed presence.</p>
  <p><span class="aw-watch"><strong>No number is attached:</strong></span> this trigger should first appear through RFQs, followed by qualified programs 12-24 months later.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 11 · New vertical optionality</h3>
  <p>Management is exploring:</p>
  <ul><li><strong>Aerospace:</strong> transferring precision machining, quality and ESG credentials into civil aviation supply chains.</li><li><strong>Robotics and actuators:</strong> micro-motors and actuator components for European customers.</li><li><strong>Defence/industrial gears:</strong> RACL is on BHEL's approved-vendor list and has begun supplies.</li></ul>
  <p><span class="aw-info"><strong>Classification:</strong></span> BHEL is early revenue; aerospace and robotics remain pre-revenue optionality, potentially one to two years away from a modelable business.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-trigger">
  <h3>Trigger 12 · Kubota-Escorts working-capital realignment</h3>
  <p>Kubota is shifting selected purchases from direct exports by RACL to domestic supply through Escorts. Revenue remains, but the classification moves from export to domestic sales.</p>
  <p>Domestic invoicing should settle faster, making this a modest receivables and cash-conversion tailwind rather than a P&amp;L trigger.</p>
</div>

## 4. What is already in the price?

<div class="aw-card aw-gold">
  <h3>Valuation is pricing in substantial execution</h3>
  <p>At ₹1,569, RACL traded approximately 7% below its 52-week high after rising about 62% over one year. The stock's 39.2x TTM P/E exceeded the peer-set median of roughly 28-29x, while the reported PEG ratio was 5.79x.</p>
</div>

The valuation assumes that:

1. BMW Titan and Venus enter mass production broadly on schedule.
2. Royal Enfield continues scaling toward the 10,000-20,000 monthly nomination range.
3. KTM's recovery remains intact.
4. Heat-treatment capex delivers cost, capacity and ESG benefits.
5. The FY27 revenue range is achieved without a material margin reversal.

### Potential positive surprises

- FY27 revenue materially exceeds the upper end of ₹565 crore ±5%.
- A second Royal Enfield program converts.
- Additional ZF-linked nominations move beyond discussion.
- KTM contributes above the conservative base assumption.
- BHEL/defence, aerospace or robotics programs become commercially meaningful.

## 5. Key risks

<div class="aw-card aw-red">
  <h3>1 · Simultaneous execution</h3>
  <p>BMW, Royal Enfield, Kawasaki, ZF, BRP and the heat-treatment rebuild converge in FY27-FY28. Delays in customer approvals, equipment commissioning or capacity balancing could compress the ramp.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-card aw-red">
  <h3>2 · Cash conversion and free cash flow</h3>
  <p>Free cash flow was negative over three-, five- and seven-year windows despite PAT growth. Inventory days reached 373, receivable days 117 and the cash-conversion cycle 331 days. Renewed debt may be needed for capex.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-card aw-red">
  <h3>3 · Tax-rate volatility</h3>
  <p>Q1 FY27 effective tax rate reached approximately 47%, versus full-year guidance of 25.62%, due to deferred-tax and timing effects. Quarterly PAT can therefore remain volatile.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-card aw-red">
  <h3>4 · Customer concentration</h3>
  <p>BMW, Royal Enfield and ZF are strategically important relationships. RACL's policy of capping one customer's revenue contribution near 20% lowers concentration but can also slow individual ramps.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-card aw-red">
  <h3>5 · Commodity, currency and policy</h3>
  <p>Raw-material and energy inflation remain relevant. The RoDTEP incentive rate was cut by approximately half from FY27, with an estimated annual impact near ₹1 crore. Rupee depreciation offers a partial offset.</p>
</div>

<div class="aw-card aw-red">
  <h3>6 · Valuation</h3>
  <p>At 39.2x TTM earnings, the stock embeds a successful ramp and continued margin strength. A soft quarter, slower validation, renewed leverage or sector de-rating could compress the multiple materially.</p>
</div>
